# On the farm (`farm`)

Code: `src/scenes/recipes/farm.tsx`. Exact sizes, counts and colours: [reference](reference.md#on-the-farm-farm). First appears at **Level 10**. Defined with the [recipe questionnaire](../recipe-questionnaire.md).

## Bands

| Band | Height | How things sit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In the sky | 0–24% | `air`, 1 row |
| By the barn | 24–38% | backdrop only: hills and the barn |
| In the field | 38–55.5% | `ground`, 1 row, at most 2 kinds; the tractor drives one way |
| In the farmyard | 58.5–100% | `ground`, 2 rows |

A fence runs between the field and the farmyard (55.5–58.5%).

## Things and their habits

- **Sky:** sun (one, not counted), crows, hot-air balloons.
- **Field:**
  - **tractor** (extra large, one at most, red, green, blue or yellow; faces the way it drives)
  - **scarecrows** (shirt colour asked; two are spread apart)
  - pumpkins (countable), hay bales. Sheep stay in the farmyard with the other animals, never among the crops.
- **Farmyard:**
  - **cows** (large; black and white or brown) and **horses** (large), spread out, never in a tight row
  - pigs, goats, sheep, chickens (white or brown; can bunch together)
  - a dog, the farmer (straw hat; shirt colour asked), a wheelbarrow
  - **milk churns** at the front of the yard, hay bales spread out.
- **Black sheep:** on levels with mixed colours, a flock can have black and white sheep ("What colours were the sheep?" → "Black and white"). Sheep have grey faces and legs so a black sheep still reads as a sheep.

## Backdrop

- **Background:** sky, rolling hills, the barn, a field with crop rows, a white fence, and a grass farmyard.
- **The barn** stands on the left or right (chosen per picture) and is red, blue, green or yellow (chosen per picture). Its colour is a **detail fact**: "What colour was the barn?" is asked on levels with detail questions (Level 8+, Medium and Hard), like the city's house numbers.
- **Marks**, kept clear of every thing: white clouds (solid), grass tufts (30%), and a line along the front of the fence (25%).

## Colour schemes

`summer` (blue sky, green field, grass farmyard) and `harvest` (peach sky, golden hills, sage field, olive farmyard). The farmyard is grass rather than sand on purpose: golden hay, pink pigs and silver milk churns didn't stand out enough on a sandy yard, so the contrast rule never placed them. The harvest field is sage so orange pumpkins and every tractor colour show.

## Questions it gives

Counts and colours of everything, positions ("In the field, on the right"), "What was in the farmyard, on the left?" when only one kind is there, in / NOT in the picture, the barn's colour, and the sheep's colours on Hard.
