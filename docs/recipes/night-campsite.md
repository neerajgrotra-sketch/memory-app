## Night and contrast

Dark things vanish on the dark sky and grass, so:

- **The sky has pale things only:** the moon, barn owls, a shooting star, a plane. No bats: they'd be invisible.
- **Camping gear comes in bright colours** (tents, jumpers, sleeping bags), and the contrast rules drop any colour that wouldn't show.
- **The log seat and firewood are lit by the fire:** lighter than wood looks by day. At first they never appeared, because dark brown on dark grass failed the contrast check.

Light: the sky lightens towards the horizon, and the fire's warm glow is strongest in front and fades out across the pitches (soft gradients, not hard bands).
