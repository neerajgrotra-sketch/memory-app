# Under the sea (`under-sea`)

Code: `src/scenes/recipes/underSea.tsx`. Exact sizes, counts and colours: [reference](reference.md#under-the-sea-under-sea). First appears at **Level 12**. Defined with the [recipe questionnaire](../recipe-questionnaire.md).

## Bands

| Band | Height | How things sit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (sky) | 0–5% | backdrop only, above the wavy surface |
| Near the surface | 7.5–30% | `air`, 1 row: things that breathe air or drift |
| In the open water | 31–63% | `air`, 2 rows |
| On the seabed | 68–100% | `ground`, 2 rows, on the sand |

## Things and their habits

- **Near the surface:** a whale (extra large, one at most, not counted), dolphins (shared with the beach), sea turtles, jellyfish (pink, purple or white). Whales and dolphins never go deeper: they breathe air.
- **Open water:**
  - fish (orange, yellow, pink, purple or red; a school can be **different colours on Hard**: "What colours were the fish?")
  - clownfish (always orange with white stripes, so they're a different thing from "fish")
  - a shark (one at most; light grey, because a mid grey didn't stand out on blue water)
  - divers (a grown-up person, bigger than the fish; wetsuit colour asked), a submarine (one at most)
  - turtles and jellyfish can also be here.
- **Seabed:** an octopus, crabs and starfish (shared with the beach), seashells, coral, and **seaweed**: tall, so it grows from the sand and rises into the open water; a few are spread apart.

## Backdrop

- **Background:** a sliver of sky above a wavy surface, water getting darker with depth, slanting sunlight rays (faint), a rolling sandy seabed with rocks at the edges.
- **The treasure chest** sits in a bottom corner, left or right (chosen per picture), and the corner is kept clear so nothing hides it. Each picture chooses what's in it: gold coins, pearls, red jewels (lid thrown back, treasure heaped up, a couple of pieces spilled) or nothing (lid shut).
- **Detail fact:** "What was in the treasure chest?" with those four answers, asked on levels with detail questions (Level 8+, Medium and Hard). An open/closed question would only have two answers; every question needs four.
- **Marks:** rising bubbles (35%) and sand ripples (30%), kept clear of every thing.

## Colour schemes

`tropical` (turquoise) and `deep` (darker blue).

## Questions it gives

Counts and colours of everything, positions ("In the open water, on the left"), "What was near the surface, on the right?" when only one kind is there, in / NOT in the picture, the diver's wetsuit, the fish's colours on Hard, and what was in the treasure chest.
