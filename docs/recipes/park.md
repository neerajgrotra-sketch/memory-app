## Scale

Everything is sized against the shared person (1.7 m tall, so 1 m ≈ 0.115 of the picture width): a bench 1.8 m long (0.21), a bicycle with its rider 1.7 m (0.20), a swan 1.2 m (0.14), the rowing boat 3 m (0.34), the swings, slide and fountain 2.5–3 m (0.28), the park clock 3.5 m tall, a tree about 4 m tall.

**Left out on purpose:** ducks, balls and butterflies. Anything under about 1 m would have to be drawn at least 44 pt wide, which makes it look giant next to people (the lesson from the supermarket).

## Things and their habits

- **What's happening:** one dog on its own carries a ball, a stick, a frisbee or nothing; one person on their own may wear a sun hat, a cap or a woolly hat and hold a balloon, an ice cream or a shopping bag; each bicycle is ridden or parked (story questions, from Level 1).

- **Sky:** the sun, birds, kites, hot-air balloons, a plane (all shared).
- **Pond:** a rowing boat with someone rowing (extra wide, one at most, with a **number** on its side), swans, a stone fountain (one).
- **Path:** bicycles, each ridden or parked on its stand (frame colour asked; "Was anyone riding the bicycle?"), an ice-cream cart (umbrella colour asked; it can also stand on the grass), people and dogs.
- **Grass:** benches (against the back, spread apart), picnic blankets with a basket, flower beds (flower colour asked), a swing set, a slide (red, blue or orange), the **park clock** on a tall post, trees (tall, spread apart; they rise over the path behind them), people and dogs.

## Detail facts

- **"What time was on the park clock?"** Asked on every level whenever the clock is there, with times that get more exact as you level up (see [Questions](../questions.md#clocks-living-room-city-park)).
- **"What number was on the rowing boat?"** (1–24), from Level 8 like house numbers, whenever the boat is there. The number plate is drawn large enough to read at a glance. Wrong answers are one or two along, the digits swapped, or ten more.

## Colour schemes

`summer` and `spring`: light green grass so the trees stand out, a gravel path, a blue pond, and a line of trees on the far bank. Marks: clouds, ripples on the pond, grass tufts.
